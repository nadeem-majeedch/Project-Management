## Common wrong answers

| Error | Correction |
|---|---|
| Students listed once as a row with P=5 | thousands of people aren't a stakeholder with power 5 — separate users (S3, low power high interest) from union (S4) and council (S7) |
| Vendor marked low interest "obviously" | unverified assumption; Lab 2 brief asks what evidence would settle it — pass requires the question, not the assumption |
| No non-person entity | missed the pattern that rules/contracts behave as actors |
| Gap plan = "improve communication" | not an action; needs who/what/when |

## Reflection guidance

1. Typical both-missed: exams office or academic council — registers fail
   where power is quiet until the deadline.
2. S3/S4 interest spikes at result publishing (week 14+) — engagement must
   start before the spike, not during.
3. Reading vendor interest as low risks missing schema-change preparation;
   evidence that settles it: their change-request history, SOW clauses.
