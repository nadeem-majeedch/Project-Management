# Lab 12 Answer Key — Communication Plan

## Model communication matrix (needs-rows; accept equivalents)

| ID | Need | Audience | Channel / cadence | Owner | Artifact | Feedback loop |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | weekly confidence read | Registrar | 30-min, weekly | PM | one-page RAG | asks answered ≤ 2 days |
| C2 | early warning on bad news | Registrar | escalation memo, event-driven ≤ 24 h | PM | situation/complication/question/answer memo | sponsor call ≤ 24 h |
| C3 | SLA evidence confidence | Exams office | gate briefings (B/D/G) | QA lead | rehearsal + audit evidence pack | joint sign-off at G |
| C4 | schema-change visibility | LMS vendor | biweekly sync + change log | PM | change log + CR register | written response ≤ 5 days |
| C5 | outage/status honesty | Students (users) | status page + union channel | PM | posted notices, post-rollout | union feedback item in CCB |
| C6 | progress + asks | Student union | monthly open demo | PM | demo + question log | questions answered in next demo |
| C7 | resource decisions | IT head | biweekly 1:1 | PM | capacity report | staffing fixes ≤ 1 week |

## Channel rules (model — pass requires all four, concrete)

- **Escalation ladder:** dev → tech lead (same day) → PM (≤ 24 h) → sponsor
  (≤ 48 h, or immediately for SLA/quality threats). Each rung has an SLA and
  an owner; skipping rungs allowed for 48-hour-rule threats.
- **n+1:** every problem memo carries ≥ 1 option with cost/benefit. Exempt:
  active incidents (incident process, not options, first).
- **Worse-earlier:** confirmed bad news reaches registrar within 24 h —
  before the weekly slot, not at it. Test: "would I rather she heard this
  from me or in the corridor?"
- **Meeting hygiene:** agenda 24 h before; decisions + owners out within 24 h
  after; every meeting produces its artifact.

## Governance artifacts (model contents)

- **RAG dashboard:** the four views from the monitoring template, thresholds
  stated (a red without a threshold number is a rejected dashboard).
- **CCB minutes:** decision, CR number, options considered, **recorded
  dissent**, effective date (Lab 15 reuses this format).
- **Change log:** after each CCB — what moved, effect on cost/finish, who
  approved.
- **Escalation memo:** SCQA format, ≤ 1 page, sent ≤ 24 h after
  confirmation.

## Failure-mode table (model)

| Failure mode | Prevention in this plan |
|---|---|
| Sponsor hears first from anyone else | C2 worse-earlier ≤ 24 h; registrar on all incident lists |
| Hallway decisions | CCB-only scope changes; hallway input goes to CCB as options |
| Students hear from outsiders first | C5/C6: status page + union demo before union can break news |
| Survey/meeting fatigue | needs-based rows only; every artifact has a consumer and an owner |

## Common wrong answers

| Error | Correction |
|---|---|
| One row per stakeholder | needs-first; the registrar has ≥ 2 needs (confidence + warning) |
| Feedback loop column empty | communication is two-directional or it is broadcasting |
| "Communicate better" as rule | rules have SLAs, owners, formats |
| Governance = dashboard only | CCB minutes, change log, escalation memos are the audit trail |
| Students ignored as audience | 2018's lesson; the union observer is the channel |

## Reflection guidance

1. C2 (worse-earlier ≤ 24 h) is the corridor-trust killer's antidote —
   quote students who wrote it with the 24-h number.
2. Aggregation strategy: users → status page + union as representative
   channel, owned by PM; the union observer seat closes the loop into CCB.
3. n+1 cost is wrong to pay when options genuinely don't exist yet (raw
   discovery) — the rule then degrades honestly to "options by [date]",
   which is still worse-earlier compliant.

## Preparation notes

Grade rules, not intentions. Strong submissions quote numbers (SLAs) and
formats (SCQA). Lab 15's CCB minutes reuse this format verbatim — say so.
