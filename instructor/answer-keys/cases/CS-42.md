# Answer key — CS-42 · AI-drafted risk register critique

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Classify-then-gate

## Reasoning

Hallucinated entries fail one test: no verifiable referent in the project (the discontinued vendor product). Generic entries ('scope creep may occur') fail specificity: no cause-effect pair this project would recognize. The misframed opportunity inverts sign — a register that hides an opportunity distorts response planning as much as a phantom threat. The process rule: 'AI-drafted risks enter the register only with a named human owner who can state the evidence trail; every entry gets a verification tag (verified / assumption / unverified) and unverified entries are reviewed at the next risk meeting or dropped; confidence language is rewritten as conditional.' This keeps drafting speed while making the human owner accountable for referents.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Banning AI for risk drafting: defensible for regulated contexts; the faster failure is unverified registers, which humans also produce.
## Facilitation

Have students run their own last retrospective risk through the rule — verification discipline gets personal fast.
