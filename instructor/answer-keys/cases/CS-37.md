# Answer key — CS-37 · Delivering bad news early

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Decision-first disclosure

## Reasoning

Message: one paragraph of fact (what was found, why it's certain), the decision needed (choose recovery option), the option set with costs, the ask (15 minutes Thursday, before Friday's deck is rebuilt). Sequence: team same-day (they own the facts); manager Tuesday PM (no surprise cascade); sponsor Wednesday with the page — 'early' means as soon as the shape of the decision is clear, not after every number is polished. Follow-up: weekly one-slide status against the chosen option for four weeks, then normal cadence; the first on-time report after bad news does more for trust than ten clean weeks before it.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Waiting for Friday's meeting to disclose formally: protects no one, converts a three-week problem into a trust problem; indefensible here.
## Facilitation

Compare two drafts: one opening with history, one with the ask; ask sponsors-in-the-room which they'd rather receive.
