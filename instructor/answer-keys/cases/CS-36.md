# Answer key — CS-36 · Negotiating a scope cut with the sponsor

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Option-framed negotiation

## Reasoning

The 'nothing can go' stance survives because nobody has shown what each option costs in the sponsor's own currency. Option framing: (1) reporting module to phase 3 — lose: exec dashboards at go-live; protect: all clinical workflow. (2) UAT depth cut — lose: defect safety net; the risk must be named (finding P1s in production at a hospital). (3) Budget add — lose: money, the sponsor's scarcest visible resource. Opening: 'To protect the go-live date, here are three ways to find four weeks — I recommend the first; let me show you why.' The objection response concedes the principle ('all three matter') and relocates the choice to which loss the sponsor prefers.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Lead with the recommendation and ask for objections: faster, works with a decisive sponsor; the three-option brief stays as the backup pack.
## Facilitation

Have pairs run the opening; most students open with the problem, not the options — the reframe is the skill.
