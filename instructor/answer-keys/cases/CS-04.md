# Answer key — CS-04 · Sorting 20 artifacts into PMBOK 7 domains

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Purpose-based classification

## Reasoning

Classify by the question the artifact answers, not its format: a burndown answers 'are we delivering?' (delivery performance), a model card answers 'is the system appropriate and harmless?' (measurement and stewardship), a rollback plan answers 'can we recover?' (uncertainty). The classic confusions are stakeholder vs team (a RACI serves both), planning vs delivery (a release plan bridges both), and measurement vs delivery (a CFD is a delivery instrument that measures flow).

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- None recorded.
## Facilitation

Run as a card-sort race, then debrief only the contested cards; contested cards teach more than clean ones.
