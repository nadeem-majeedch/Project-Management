# Answer key — CS-44 · Regulatory-driven predictive migration

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Phase-gated parallel migration

## Reasoning

Phases: (1) shadow deployment — predictive service runs alongside rules, no clinical exposure, comparison logged; (2) parallel decision — both produce recommendations, clinicians see rules with predictive annotations; (3) supervised cutover — predictive primary for the three zero-downtime accounts last, with instant rule fallback. Parallel-run exit threshold: agreement rate within tolerance over 60 consecutive days with discrepancy review complete. Rollback triggers: agreement drop, audit finding, or incident severity P1 — invocable by the on-call clinical safety officer without committee approval; speed is the point of a rollback trigger.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Direct cutover with heavy pre-validation: faster, acceptable where parallel running is impractical; here the zero-downtime accounts and audit evidence make parallel the honest choice.
## Facilitation

Ask who owns discrepancy triage during parallel run — unowned discrepancy queues are where migrations stall.
