# Answer key — CS-60 · Hybrid governance for the ML platform

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Two-lane hybrid with an interlock queue

## Reasoning

Agile lane: platform engineering, pipeline changes, tooling, experiments — two-week sprints, DoD as quality floor. Gated lane: model releases, schema changes to served features, anything touching consent scope — each passes a gate with evidence (model card, slice evaluation, lineage sign-off). Interlock: sprint output lands in a gate queue reviewed every Monday by the model release board (model owner, governance delegate, product lead); five-day decision SLA forces evidence to be prepared before the sprint ends, not after. Where hybrids break: the handoff — sprint teams promise releases on a calendar the gate queue doesn't honor. Fix: the sprint commitment includes gate-queue lead time as explicitly as it includes engineering time. Deadlock authority: product lead decides, governance delegate's objection is recorded and escalated, never silently dropped.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Full SAFe-style release train: adds ceremony the 12-person platform doesn't need; the two-lane design is the minimum that satisfies all three rhythms.
## Facilitation

Ask what happens to gate evidence quality when the gate SLA is five days — the SLA is what makes the evidence real.
