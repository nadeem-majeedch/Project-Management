# Answer key — CS-31 · Feature-first to hypothesis-first backlog rewrite

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Falsifiable backlog

## Reasoning

Example rewrite: 'WhatsApp channel' → 'Assumption: churned-risk customers respond on WhatsApp better than email; test: 200-customer pilot; signal: response rate; threshold: ≥25% vs email 12%; kill: <15% after two batches.' The real-time scoring item is the kill candidate: its cost (streaming infra) dwarfs any tested signal at current batch latency — the honest move is to test value of faster scores on a daily batch first. Ordering by information value per cost front-loads cheap, decisive tests.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Building WhatsApp regardless (sponsor mandate): name it as an override, log the untested assumption, and design the cheapest measurement around it.
## Facilitation

Grade the kill criterion hardest; unfalsifiable hypotheses are the tell of feature-thinking.
