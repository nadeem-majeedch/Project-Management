# Answer key — CS-34 · Team working agreement

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Dysfunction-mapped norms

## Reasoning

Silent disagreement → 'disagree in the room: each decision point gets one explicit round of objections before moving on; objections after the meeting reopen the decision, not the decision-maker.' Carry member → 'integration is a named rotating role each sprint, selected at planning; the name is on the board.' Overrun → 'timeboxes end with a decision, a defer, or an owner for the follow-up — no fourth option.' Enforcement: the retro reviews violations by pattern, not by name; two repeats of one item in a month triggers a 15-minute agenda slot to fix the system, not the person. Review: every second retro, one item is retired or rewritten.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- None recorded.
## Facilitation

Ban the word 'respect' from drafts; norms that can't be violated can't be enforced.
