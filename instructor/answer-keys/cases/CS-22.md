# Answer key — CS-22 · Monte Carlo on a class schedule

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Histogram-to-decision analysis

## Reasoning

P(on-time) = cumulative trials at ≤16 weeks ÷ 10,000 = 64%. The median is the bracket where cumulative first crosses 5,000 — the 16-week bracket. Late-trial mean overrun uses only the 3,600 late trials (1×2100 + 2×1000 + 3×400 + 4×100)/3600 = 1.58 weeks; unconditional mean = 5,700/10,000 = 0.57 weeks — the delay most projects will actually experience, far smaller than the late-projects' pain suggests, and the gap between the two numbers is exactly why survivorship-biased reporting misleads. P70 sits in the 17-week bracket, so the commitment date is 17 weeks with the residual 30% risk named. P50 as a commitment fails half the time by construction — sponsors remember the misses, not the method.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Committing to 16 with a contingency reserve: defensible if the reserve is sponsor-controlled and explicitly drawn; the simulation says reserve ≈ 1.5 weeks (late mean).
