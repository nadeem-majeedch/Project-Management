# Answer key — CS-02 · Success vs failure twin projects

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Critical-success-factor reconstruction

## Reasoning

Usage and longevity differences point upstream to the two decisions that shape demand: who the sponsor is (funding continuity) and what 'done' meant for v1 (scope fit to real analyst workflow). Data-quality handling explains the 14-month delay but not the decommission; hence ranking: sponsor > done-definition > user interviews > demo cadence > data-quality handling.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Data-quality-first ranking: defensible if you argue bad data killed trust, but decommission after only 3 months suggests demand, not data, was the fatal gap.
## Facilitation

Ask teams to defend opposite rankings for two minutes each; the point is that ranking requires an explicit causal theory.
