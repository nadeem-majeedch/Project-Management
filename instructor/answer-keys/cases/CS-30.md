# Answer key — CS-30 · WIP limits as throttles

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Little's Law paths

## Reasoning

Current cycle time = 18/6 = 3 weeks. Path A: WIP = 2 × 6 = 12 items — cut 6 items of WIP (finishing before starting; the cost is saying 'no' mid-flight, plus stakeholder impatience). Path B: throughput = 18/2 = 9/week — a 50% gain with fixed headcount; unrealistic without removing blockers. Recommend A: it is within the team's control, and lower WIP typically improves quality (less context switching, fresher feedback). Little's Law assumes stability; a team with wildly varying arrival rates needs smoothing before the arithmetic means anything.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Path B via automation investment: defensible if a bottleneck analysis shows mechanical waste; otherwise it's a hope, not a plan.
