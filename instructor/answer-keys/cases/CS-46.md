# Answer key — CS-46 · Crash economics on the streaming upgrade

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Slope-ordered crashing with CP tracking

## Reasoning

Slopes: A = (55,000−40,000)/(4−3) = 15,000/wk; B = (105,000−90,000)/1 = 15,000/wk; C = (90,000−70,000)/1 = 20,000/wk; D = (50,000−30,000)/1 = 20,000/wk. Baseline paths: A–C = 9 wk; A–B–D = 13 wk (critical). Step 1: crash B (cheapest, on CP) → A–B–D = 12. Step 2: still A–B–D critical (A–C = 9); crash A (15,000) → A–B–D = 11, A–C = 8. Total direct increase = PKR 30,000 for two weeks. Indirect saving = 16,000, so net compression cost is 14,000 — and the deadline risk removed may be worth more than either. Crashing C or D (slope 20,000) first is the classic error: they are off the critical path and shorten nothing.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Fast-track instead of crash: free but adds rework risk (see CS-53); worth stating as the comparison the sponsor will ask for.
