# Answer key — CS-83 · Splitting the model team mid-flight

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Commitment-protected split

## Reasoning

Sequencing: the researchers stay on feature engineering until the quarterly model ships — the split is organizational on day one (new reporting lines) and physical at the commitment boundary; announce it that way to prevent the two-week walking-out-the-door effect. Transfer evidence: each feature pipeline component gets a run-book plus a supervised handover run where the receiving engineer executes a full retrain from scratch — the retrain succeeding is the transfer, not the document. Where knowledge is genuinely two-person deep, the plan names it and adds one sprint of pairing. Re-negotiation script: 'The commitment stands with the current team through ship. After ship, research support for the next cycle changes — here is the capacity impact and the two options (one-cycle slip or scope reduction on exploratory features).' The commercial visibility cuts both ways: it pressures you, and it pressures the sponsor to accept a staged split — use the same fact they bring.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Immediate physical split: cleaner organizationally, and it risks the commitment with a team that just lost its feature knowledge — indefensible given the external visibility.
## Facilitation

Ask what happens if the retrain fails during handover — that failure mode is the reason 'supervised retrain' beats 'signed acknowledgment' as transfer evidence.
