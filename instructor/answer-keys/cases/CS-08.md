# Answer key — CS-08 · One-page charter under pressure

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Known/unknown/invented triage

## Reasoning

A strong charter under pressure marks three states: known (complaints exist, funds exist), unknown (actual budget figure, sponsor identity, deadline), invented (anything the team supplies to look complete). The testable objective pattern: 'Reduce average service-request resolution time from X to Y by date Z' with X to be measured in week 1 — the honesty of the unmeasured baseline is the mark of a good charter, not a weakness.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Waiting for full answers before drafting: defensible in low-urgency contexts, but the exercise's point is disciplined honesty under time pressure.
## Facilitation

Grade the open-questions list more heavily than the charter prose; it reveals whether students invented facts.
