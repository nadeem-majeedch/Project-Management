# Answer key — CS-71 · Phase gates for a regulated AI feature

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Evidence-based gates with agile interior

## Reasoning

Gates: G1 data-use (consent scope, DPIA, data governance sign-off — authority: governance board, 5 days); G2 model validation (slice evaluation, model card, reproducibility — authority: model owner + clinical informatics, 5 days); G3 clinical safety (failure-mode analysis, escalation protocol, shadow-period results — authority: clinical safety officer, 5 days); G4 deployment (rollback evidence, monitoring thresholds, training sign-off — authority: sponsor, 3 days). Between gates: agile sprints on everything not gated — features, UI, pipeline, evaluation harnesses; sprint output feeds the next gate's evidence pack. Tailoring justification: the gates exist because the regulator audits evidence trails, not intentions; 'gates if asked' forfeits the one thing gates produce — evidence assembled contemporaneously rather than reconstructed after the fact. The honest tailoring is compression (fixed 5-day SLAs), not elimination.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Two gates (data-use + deployment) with internal reviews: faster; defensible only if clinical safety review is genuinely embedded in sprint DoD — usually it is not.
## Facilitation

Connect to CS-60: same interlock pattern, higher stakes; the SLA discipline is what makes gates compatible with agile anywhere.
