# Answer key — CS-39 · Four ethics dilemmas in writing

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Code-anchored proportionate action

## Reasoning

(1) Honesty violated; harm: sponsor decisions on false signal; action: report true number with a recovery plan attached — honesty without a path reads as failure. (2) Fairness; harm: bid integrity; action: decline, disclose to the evaluation lead, log it. (3) Honesty again, but the pad is a symptom of estimate distrust — action: raise estimating process, not the person; if the pad hides in a submitted baseline, it must be corrected. (4) Responsibility + respect; harm: data subjects and the organization; action: block the experiment pending consent/data-governance review — this is the inaction-is-violation case. The scripted sentence for (1): 'We're at 45, not 60 — here's what gets us back and what I need from you to do it.'

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Vignette 3 as reportable misconduct: defensible if the pad is deliberate concealment in a governed baseline; the distinction is intent plus visibility.
## Facilitation

Collect the scripted sentences anonymously and read three aloud; students remember sentences, not frameworks.
