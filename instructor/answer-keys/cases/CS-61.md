# Answer key — CS-61 · TCPI and the credibility ceiling

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** TCPI credibility analysis

## Reasoning

CPI = 2.6/3.0 = 0.8667; SPI = 2.6/2.9 = 0.8966; EAC₁ = 5,000,000/0.8667 ≈ 5,769,231; VAC ≈ −769,231. TCPI(BAC) = 2.4/2.0 = 1.20: the team must produce 1.20 of earned value per rupee for the remaining five months after producing 0.867 for seven — with CPI stable for three months, that is a credibility stretch, not a plan. TCPI(EAC₁) = 2.4/2,769,231 = 0.8667 — exactly current CPI, by construction: EAC₁ assumes current efficiency persists. Commit to EAC₁ and re-baseline the VAC; TCPI 1.20 is what you would need to believe a turnaround the trend line does not support. Briefing: 'We are 13% inefficient and 10% behind; at current efficiency we land at 5.77M, 770k over. I am committing to that number and re-planning the remaining scope against it.'

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Committing to BAC with a recovery plan: honest only if the plan changes the efficiency drivers, not the slogan — ask what specifically the plan does in month 8 that months 5–7 did not.
