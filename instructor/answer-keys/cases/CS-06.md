# Answer key — CS-06 · Life-cycle choice for five vignettes

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Volatility/cost-of-change matching

## Reasoning

(a) predictive — requirements fixed by statute, late change is unaffordable; (b) iterative — the artifact is learning, requirements emerge from findings; (c) adaptive — volatility is high and cost of change is low; (d) predictive/iterative with heavy verification — high volatility would be intolerable where failure is safety-critical; (e) hybrid — fixed half predictive, exploratory half adaptive, with an integration gate. The accepted risk: predictive accepts rework if requirements were wrong; adaptive accepts scope ambiguity at the deadline.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- (d) as adaptive with safety gates: some teams do run safety-critical adaptively; the justification must then carry the verification cost explicitly.
## Facilitation

Collect assignments in a 2×2 (volatility × cost of change) on the board; the map makes disagreements visible immediately.
