# Answer key — CS-10 · Power-interest grid and engagement plan

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Power-interest engagement differentiation

## Reasoning

High-power hostile stakeholders (records head, physician) need face-to-face interest alignment — their hostility is positional, and the records head's real fear (audit exposure) can be converted into project support by making the project improve their audit posture. The vendor account manager is low-power, high-interest: contain with formal channels and clean exit criteria rather than engagement. Engage the records head first: conversion is plausible and their power is highest.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Engage the physician first via a respected peer: defensible if a credible intermediary exists; otherwise risks reinforcing the slight.
## Facilitation

Push on the ethics line between 'aligning interests' and 'buying off' a hostile stakeholder.
