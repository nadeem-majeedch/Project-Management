# Answer key — CS-09 · Stakeholder hunt on a hospital analytics project

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Exchange-based identification

## Reasoning

Every stakeholder gives and takes something: data owners give access and take control assurances; unions give cooperation and take workload guarantees; auditors give sign-off and take evidence. Hidden stakeholders surface by walking the data flow and the approval flow, not the org chart. The go-live surprise is usually the night-shift operational staff — the actual users of reports nobody interviewed.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- None recorded.
## Facilitation

Have teams count how many of their 15 are 'people who say yes'; a list of allies is a red flag.
