# Answer key — CS-45 · Startup MVP under runway pressure

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Dual-runway analysis

## Reasoning

Base burn = 1,350k/month → runway = 6,000,000/1,350,000 = 4.44 months. Month-7 revenue means 6 months of burn must be covered: shortfall = 6 × 1,350,000 − 6,000,000 = PKR 2.1M. Contractor cut: burn 1,150k → runway 5.22 months; still short (6 × 1,150,000 − 6,000,000 = 900k gap). Neither posture reaches revenue on cash alone — the honest recommendation combines the cut with a bridge: smaller raise, revenue advance, or scope cut that pulls revenue earlier. The non-cash cost of the cut: feature X slips a month, which may be the revenue-driving feature — the analysis must name which runway the cut actually extends.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Keep contractors and raise a bridge now: preserves capability runway; costs dilution and time — defensible if feature X drives month-7 revenue.
