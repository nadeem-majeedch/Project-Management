# Answer key — CS-48 · The requirements freeze that wasn't

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Tiered freeze with clinical exception path

## Reasoning

Tiers: frozen (baseline), conditional (change allowed only by swapping equal scope, CCB-approved), open (safety and regulatory only — defined as patient-safety impact or audit requirement, evidenced in writing). Intake: every request logged in one queue with requester, rationale, safety claim; PM + clinical safety officer triage weekly. The re-anchor conversation reframes: 'every absorbed one-liner took testing capacity from the defects that could harm a patient' — the nurse's own values, not schedule optics. Absorbing changes felt safe because each was small; the compounding effect is invisible until rework surfaces, which is precisely why creep is the classic beginner failure.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Full freeze with zero exceptions: cleaner governance, but in a hospital it fails the first safety amendment and destroys the mechanism's credibility.
