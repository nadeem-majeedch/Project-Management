# Answer key — CS-51 · Committed costs and the change-order ambush

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Spend-commitment-forecast reconciliation

## Reasoning

The report compares cash out vs planned spend and ignores 1.2M of committed future outflow plus 800k of unrecorded scope: true remaining cost is understated by 2M, roughly a quarter of the budget — 'under budget' is fiction. Root cause: cost reporting keyed to invoices rather than commitments, and a CCB without a written-decision rule. Fix: (1) cost baseline maintained as committed + spent, forecast = remaining commitments + remaining estimates; (2) CCB decision rule — no implementation before a signed CR, enforced by the deployment gate; (3) month-end reconciliation of commitments ledger to contract register. The unwinding of the verbal order is political: present it as baseline hygiene, not accusation.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Absorbing the 800k into contingency: hides a scope decision in a risk pot — the exact accounting this case exists to prevent.
## Facilitation

Ask what the EVM numbers would look like — PV/EV/AC all look fine while the forecast is 2M wrong; EVM's blindness to commitments is its known gap.
