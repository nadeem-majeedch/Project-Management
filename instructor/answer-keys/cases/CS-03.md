# Answer key — CS-03 · Portfolio cut-line exercise

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Weighted scoring + budget knapsack

## Reasoning

Weighted score = 0.5·alignment + 0.3·impact + 0.2·feasibility; then a budget knapsack, not a rank-order cut. The trap: the highest-scoring project (P1, 4.1) does NOT belong in the optimal bundle — it consumes 6.5M for 4.1 points, and every bundle containing P1 scores at most 11.3, while {P2, P3, P4, P6} scores 14.1 at 10.0M. Rank-order funding walks into exactly this trap. Value-per-million is the tiebreaker when two candidate bundles cost the same.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Rank-order funding: simpler and easier to defend politically, but it funds P1 first and strands the cheap-high-value projects — 11.3 points against the optimal 14.1.
- Cost-efficiency funding: maximizes value per rupee but can fund only small projects and starve strategic bets.
