# Answer key — CS-62 · Escalating a failing vendor without killing the delivery

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Escalation ladder with parallel-path insurance

## Reasoning

Ladder: formal performance notice citing the SLA ledger (three months of credits = documented breach pattern); 60-day cure period with a named vendor executive sponsor and weekly joint metrics; exit trigger = any two further misses in the cure window. Parallel-path: stand up the buyer-side synthetic probe suite (already built for SLA measurement in CS-25) against a candidate replacement in shadow mode — 60 days of shadow data makes exit executable instead of theoretical. Credits fail because they price inconvenience, not dependency: 120k credits against a product whose revenue rides on the API is not a remedy. The parallel-path's cost is the insurance premium; it is cheaper than the first month of a stalled migration.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Terminating immediately: legally available, operationally reckless without shadow data — the case's facts (delivery dependency) make it indefensible.
## Facilitation

Ask what the vendor's executive sponsor changes — accountability, not engineering; the metrics stay identical.
