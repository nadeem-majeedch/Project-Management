# Answer key — CS-55 · Facilitating the risk workshop that doesn't bore

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Pre-mortem with structured airtime

## Reasoning

Agenda: 10 min pre-mortem framing ('it is 6 months out and the project failed — write the story'), 25 min silent writing then round-robin reading (each voice once before discussion; anonymity via numbered cards read aloud by the facilitator), 30 min clustering and cause-chaining (generic risks become specific when forced to name cause-effect), 15 min P×I quick-score, 10 min owners assigned live. Follow-through: register updated within 48h with contributor credit; triage at next weekly; each contributor gets the disposition of their item — the loop, not the pizza, is what makes people contribute next time. Pre-mortem works because it licenses pessimism as an intellectual task rather than disloyalty.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Anonymous digital intake instead of live round-robin: better for very hierarchical teams, loses the cause-chaining discussion; hybrid is the honest answer.
## Facilitation

Run the pre-mortem opening on this course itself for five minutes; students generate better risks about their own capstone in that frame.
