# Answer key — CS-89 · Pricing the consent re-platform

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** EMV with governance overlay

## Reasoning

Expected impact = 800,000 + 0.6×500,000 + 0.4×2,000,000 = 800,000 + 300,000 + 800,000 = 1,900,000. Unmitigated annual = 0.15 × 1,900,000 = 285,000. Mitigated = 0.05 × 1,900,000 + 150,000 = 95,000 + 150,000 = 245,000. Net saving: 40,000/year — thin. The arithmetic alone says 'marginal'; the decision is not marginal: the 0.15 probability is an estimate of a distribution with a fat tail (the 2.0M fine has a sibling at 5.0M if the regulator finds the audit trail was ignored — CS-85 documents exactly that trail); consent scope is a rights question where expected-value framing underweights the harm to data subjects; and the audit finding makes 'we calculated it was cheaper' a public-position liability. Recommendation: proceed with the re-platform — the 40k is the wrong margin to optimize, the tail dominates the risk, and the governance posture (F1 already critical in CS-85) requires the fix regardless. The arithmetic's honest role: it shows the mitigation is affordable, not that it is mandatory — the mandatory comes from the governance analysis.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Deferring one year on the 40k logic: saves 40k, carries 285k expected exposure plus the audit-finding optics — the numbers argue against the deferral they seem to invite.
