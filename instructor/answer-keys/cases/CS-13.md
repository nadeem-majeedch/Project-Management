# Answer key — CS-13 · Scope-creep spotting from a change log

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Baseline-impact classification

## Reasoning

Legitimate change alters the baseline through control (the regulatory format). Creep enters without control (the extra data source added 'while we were in there'). Gold plating adds unrequested value (the data scientist's improvement — rework risk, undocumented behavior, and a model-comparison burden). The two process failures: no impact-assessment gate, and no owner for unrequested work. The rule: 'No baseline change without a recorded impact note approved by the change owner.'

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- None recorded.
## Facilitation

The gold-plating debate is the richest; let students argue whether to keep the improvement.
