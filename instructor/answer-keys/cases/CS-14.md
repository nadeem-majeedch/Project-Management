# Answer key — CS-14 · Planning-poker calibration for a capstone backlog

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** PERT mean + signed-error calibration

## Reasoning

PERT mean (O+4M+L)/6; signed error = actual − estimate. A consistent positive signed error means systematic optimism (apply a multiplicative correction); high variance with zero mean means poor spread (improve the O/L conversation, don't pad). The correction factor is the ratio of total actual to total estimate — apply it as a planning multiplier, not a per-item fudge.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- No correction, better conversation instead: defensible long-term, but a running multiplier is the honest interim fix for a known-biased team.
