# Answer key — CS-21 · Heat-map thresholds on trial

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Dual-ranking reconciliation

## Reasoning

Scores and bands follow mechanically from P×I; EMV from probability × money. The heat map ranks by ordinal severity; EMV ranks by expected loss. They disagree when a medium-banded risk has a high probability or a large money impact — ordinal scales compress tails. The register as given puts sensor drift (band high, EMV 1.0M) top by both, but key-engineer-exit (band medium, EMV 450k) outranks vendor-API-change (band medium, EMV 280k) on money while looking similar on the map. The lesson: banding drives conversation priority; EMV drives reserve sizing.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Keep heat map as sole ranking: defensible for communication simplicity, but the sponsor's challenge in this case is precisely about exposure, so EMV must enter.
