# Answer key — CS-27 · Sponsor dashboard and mock CCB

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Decision-framing dashboard + disciplined CCB

## Reasoning

Zones: milestone strip (where we are), EVM strip (CPI/SPI trend), risk top-5 with movement, CR queue with age and impact, decisions-needed-this-week. The regulator CR: approve and re-plan around it (scope-for-scope swap if needed). The competitor feature: defer with a dated decision point — strategic value is real but unquantified. The refactor: split — approve the portion touching code the regulator change will modify anyway (piggyback economics), defer the rest. Deadlock: chair decides, sponsor informed — governance fails when deadlocks are silent.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Approve all three (overcommit): the common failure; dashboard's CR-age zone exists to make queue cost visible and resist it.
## Facilitation

Score the decision log, not the role-play theater — auditability is the CCB's product.
