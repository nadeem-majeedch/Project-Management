# Answer key — CS-54 · Designing the EVM cadence

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Type-based measurement with exception triggers

## Reasoning

Milestone packages: 0/100 or weighted milestones, earned by verifiable completion evidence — immune to gaming by design. Hourly packages: earned hours from timesheet against baseline (EV = hours performed × rate), not self-assessed percent; evidence is the timesheet, audited monthly. Exceptions forcing interim report: CPI or SPI moves >0.05 month-over-month, any package >20% over its budget-at-completion forecast, two consecutive months of SPI <0.9. Anti-gaming: earned hours reconcile to timesheets (owner: PMO analyst); milestone evidence attached in the repository (owner: QA lead); the 0.05 trigger removes the incentive to smooth bad news across months. CPI trends certify cost efficiency history; they do not certify scope completeness — scope verification stays a separate gate.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- 0/100 everywhere: maximally game-proof, but buries in-flight work and makes the trend line blind — the 60% hourly work needs proportional earning.
## Facilitation

Show a pencil-whipped percent-complete curve next to a timesheet-earned curve; the divergence IS the lesson.
