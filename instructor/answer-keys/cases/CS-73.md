# Answer key — CS-73 · Drawing down the contingency reserve

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Risk-linked draw-down

## Reasoning

Policy: draws reference a register risk ID; evidence = remaining exposure after the funded response; authorization ≤50k PM, ≤150k sponsor, >150k CCB; every draw updates the risk register (response funded) and the forecast. Rulings: residency rework (150k) — approved; it funds the top register risk's response (sponsor authorizes, at the limit). Sensor drift (90k) — approved; risk was scored and EMV'd in CS-21; spending 90k against a 1.0M EMV exposure is rational. The 60k 'scope addition as risk response' — rejected: it references no risk; if it genuinely mitigates one, name the risk and the causal mechanism; otherwise it is a CR through the change process. The 25% trigger: below 100k remaining, the sponsor receives a funded-mitigation review — either the remaining risks are covered by the residue plus management reserve, or scope/schedule adjustments are opened. Unlabeled reserve spending is the best single predictor of failure because it means the control that separates risk money from wish money has already dissolved.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Management reserve as the release valve for the 60k: correct mechanism, wrong governance if the sponsor approves it informally — same money, now traceable.
## Facilitation

Ask who at GRIDSENSE knows the true reserve balance; if the answer is 'PM and nobody else', ask what that PM can be accused of in month 9.
