# Answer key — CS-90 · The compliance test that was marked green

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Escalation with correction, not ambush

## Reasoning

Obligations: the professional codes (PMI, engineering) make honesty and responsibility non-negotiable; the regulatory regime makes a false submission the organization's legal exposure regardless of intent; employment law protects the messenger only if the messenger is precise. Sequence: (1) same day — verify the facts yourself (the two failed cases, the submitted result); (2) the test lead — the first sentence: 'Help me understand the green mark on the two failed cases' — correction begins with the person who can fix it fastest; a corrected submission within days is a different regulatory event than a discovered one; (3) if not corrected in 24h — your manager and the compliance officer, with the evidence; (4) the regulator is informed by the organization through counsel — a voluntary correction is categorically different from a discovered falsehood. Contingencies: overruled by the test lead → step 3; overruled by management → compliance officer directly, then the external channel exists and using it becomes proportionate. Proportionality: the test lead's pressure is mitigation for the person (coaching, possibly removal from sign-off authority), never for the act (the submission must be corrected). 'The failures were minor' is dangerous because it argues the falsification was harmless — but the regulator now cannot trust any green mark, and trust is the thing that was actually submitted.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Immediate external disclosure skipping internal correction: fastest to clean hands, and it converts a correctable error into a maximum-severity event for the patients whose system deployment it delays — the sequence exists to fix the record fast, not to launder your role.
## Facilitation

Run the first-sentence drill; students who open with accusation trigger defensiveness and lose the 24-hour correction window that makes everything after it unnecessary.
