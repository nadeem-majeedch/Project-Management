# Answer key — CS-43 · Capstone defense simulation

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Seam-targeted rehearsal

## Reasoning

Defenses fail at seams: the resource the schedule assumes but staffing doesn't provide; the risk whose mitigation isn't budgeted; the estimate whose basis is one expert's guess. Panel questions should probe exactly these — 'walk me from your top risk to the budget line that funds its response' is the single most diagnostic question in the bank. Credible answers name the limitation before the panel does: 'our schedule assumes one DBA and we have not confirmed that allocation' outperforms a polished slide covering the same hole.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- None recorded.
## Facilitation

Keep the question bank across semesters; the same seams fail every year, which is itself a teaching asset.
