# Answer key — CS-35 · Conflict-mode case with role cards

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Interest-based release negotiation

## Reasoning

The positional fight (ship vs slip) hides interests: PM needs credibility with the sponsor (not Friday per se); tech lead needs the debt logged and time-boxed (not a veto); QA needs the untested module either tested or ring-fenced (not perfection). The collaborating move: ship Friday with the two defects documented and ring-fenced module flagged in release notes, debt items scheduled into next sprint with the tech lead owning the log — every interest substantially met. Competing (PM forcing Friday) wins the date and loses the next three commitments. Avoiding (deferring the meeting) converts a one-week decision into a month of rot.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- Slip a week: right call if the ring-fenced module touches billing; the role cards make that discoverable only through questions — the point of the exercise.
## Facilitation

Rotate roles across sections; students who play the PM defend competing, then discover its cost in debrief.
