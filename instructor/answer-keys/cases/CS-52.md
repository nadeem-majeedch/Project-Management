# Answer key — CS-52 · The milestone slip announcement

> **INSTRUCTOR ONLY — not published to the site.**

**Model answer:** Audience-tiered disclosure

## Reasoning

Matrix: vendor (day 0, PM+procurement, contract consequences and recovery options); registrar (day 0 PM, process workarounds, their questions are 'what do I do differently'); VC office (day 0-1, sponsor brief, political framing, 'who knew what when'); students (day 3, service notice signed by registrar — not the PM, students don't know the PM; their question is 'what changes for me'). Sequencing rule: those with obligations (vendor) before those with exposures (VC) before those with expectations (students); internal before external at every tier. The registrar draft leads with dates and workarounds, one paragraph of cause, zero blame — the registrar will forward it verbatim, so it must survive forwarding.

## Alternatives and evaluation criteria

- One all-hands message: efficient, and it fails all four audiences simultaneously — a classic false economy.
## Facilitation

Have students find the sentence that gets screenshotted in each draft; that sentence is the real message.
