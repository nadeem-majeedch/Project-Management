## MCQ items

### QB-U7-01 [L31 · CLO6 · Understand · F · MCQ] (1 mark)
AI-assisted project management, as framed in L31, is:
A. A replacement for the PM's judgment
B. An accelerant for drafting and analysis whose output remains human-
   verified and human-owned
C. Prohibited in this course
D. Only for schedule optimization

**Answer: B.**

### QB-U7-02 [L31 · CLO6 · Apply · I · MCQ] (1 mark)
The riskiest use of an LLM in this course's workflow is:
A. Drafting a communications plan skeleton
B. Generating the CPM forward/backward pass without verification
C. Summarizing stakeholder interviews for later human review
D. Brainstorming risk categories

**Answer: B.** (Undisclosed/unverified arithmetic — verification duty and
the course's AI contract both attach.)

## Short-answer and case items

### QB-U7-04 [L31 · CLO6 · Apply · I · short] (5 marks)
Draft the disclosure line a team should publish when an LLM drafted their
communication plan and a simulation tool produced their Monte Carlo run.

**Marking guide:** names artifact + tool class per artifact (2); states
human verification (who checked, what was changed) (2); ownership retained —
"we validated and own the result" (1). Boilerplate without specifics scores
half.

### QB-U7-05 [L31 · CLO6 · Evaluate · A · case] (6 marks)
**Case anchor: CS-90.** An AI scheduling assistant proposes compressing a
test phase to hit a contractual date, silently raising defect-escape risk.
Apply the course's AI-governance filter: what must happen before the
proposal is adopted?

**Marking criteria:** output treated as a recommendation, not a decision (2);
risk surfacing — the silent trade-off must be made explicit and priced
(defect escape vs date) (2); accountable human decision with rationale
recorded — PM, not the tool, owns the call; sponsor informed if the floor
is crossed (2).

### QB-U7-06 [L32 · CLO6 · Evaluate · A · case] (8 marks)
**Case anchor: CS-105.** A university predictive student-success service:
18 months, 6M budget, academic + regulatory governance, students/faculty/
registrar audiences. State the approach recommendation you would defend in
a capstone defense and the two strongest objections you expect.

**Marking guide:** hybrid — predictive spine (regulatory gates, semester
deployment windows) with agile model/dashboard delivery inside (3);
objection 1 — "hybrid means no commitment": answer with gates + quarterly
PI planning as commitment device (2); objection 2 — fairness/resistance:
answer with slice-based model DoD, faculty senate at the board from day
one, consent design in month 1 (3).

### QB-U7-07 [L32 · CLO6 · Evaluate · A · case] (6 marks)
**Case anchor: CS-88.** Your capstone project is simultaneously 25% over
forecast, 6 weeks late, and receives a mandatory scope addition. Produce
the constraint resolution order and the stakeholder sequence for announcing
it.

**Marking criteria:** resolution order — scope yields (defer non-mandate),
quality floor holds, budget yields once (one re-baseline), date holds last
(3); sequence — sponsor (trade-offs) → CCB (re-baseline) → team (truth
about position) → regulator (mandate date) → users (post-go-live phase)
(3). CS-88 key logic.

### QB-U7-08 [L31 · CLO6 · Apply · I · short] (4 marks)
Name two PM tasks where AI assistance is *low-risk* and two where it is
*high-risk* under the course's disclosure rules, with the distinguishing
property.

**Answer:** low-risk — drafting summaries, first-pass structures, brainstorm
expansion (verifiable prose, human owns conclusions); high-risk — any
arithmetic (CPM/EVM/EMV), decisions presented as final, anything claimed
as sourced (verification duty is highest where errors are silent).
2 + 2.

### QB-U7-09 [L32 · CLO6 · Analyze · A · short] (5 marks)
State the three questions a defense panel uses to detect template-driven
plans, and what each listens for.

**Answer:** "Why this and not the alternative?" (decisions vs choices);
"Where does this number come from?" (provenance/estimating basis);
"What breaks first if X slips?" (internal consistency under perturbation).
Any three probing questions with the listen-for satisfied; 1⅔ each.

### QB-U7-10 [L32 · CLO6 · Evaluate · A · case] (6 marks)
**Case anchor: CS-76.** You inherit a program at CPI 0.82 with a demoralized
team and a sponsor who says "tell me something I can believe." State the
first-30-days shape: what you do in week 1, your two quick wins, and the
one thing you refuse to do.

**Marking guide:** week 1 = listen and verify — one-on-ones, raw-data audit,
written statement of what is known/unknown with dates (2); quick wins =
visible control (weekly commitment ledger) + killing two zombie workstreams
(2); refuse = re-baseline in week 1 / promising a date before day 21's
assessment (2).

### QB-U7-11 [L32 · CLO6 · Remember · F · short] (3 marks)
List the capstone defense's required deliverables.

**Answer:** final master plan (PDF), defense slides ≤ 12, process evidence
export, individual contribution memos (private). ¾ each.

### QB-U7-12 [L32 · CLO6 · Analyze · A · short] (4 marks)
Why does the capstone rubric cap *internal consistency* at level 1 if the
L27 draft never existed?

**Answer:** the red-team draft is the mechanism that produces consistency —
issues surfaced and closed before the final; without it the final cannot
demonstrate the iterative correction the dimension measures (2); the cap
preserves the process-evidence principle: grades follow demonstrated
practice, not artifacts appearing from nowhere (2).

### QB-U7-13 [L31 · CLO6 · Evaluate · A · case] (5 marks)
**Case anchor: CS-92 (AI angle).** An AI tool drafts the sponsor-reset
briefing with a confident 4.2M EAC. Your own recomputation says 4.35M with
wide uncertainty. What goes to the sponsor, and what does the AI episode
teach about disclosure?

**Marking guide:** the verified number with its uncertainty, not the
confident one (2); disclosure — the draft's use is recorded; the
accountability line is the PM's recomputation and signature (2); lesson —
fluency is not accuracy; verification duty scales with consequence (1).

### QB-U7-14 [L32 · CLO6 · Evaluate · A · case] (6 marks)
**Case anchor: CS-105 (decision log).** The capstone memo appendix records
each choice, its alternative, and why the alternative lost. Why is this
appendix the highest-leverage page in the submission?

**Marking guide:** converts choices into decisions — evidence of judgment,
the thing the rubric's tailoring and Q&A dimensions measure (2); pre-arms
the defense: every Q&A probe is already answered in writing (2); auditable
integrity — rejects AI-drafted plan claims because alternatives require
reasoning the team must own (2).
