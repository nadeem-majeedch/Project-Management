## Version A

**Section A — MCQ (3 × 1 mark).**

1. The three Scrum accountabilities: (A) PM–sponsor–CCB · (B) Product
   Owner–Scrum Master–Developers · (C) architect–tester–lead ·
   (D) customer–vendor–integrator *(QB-U5-01)*
2. Cycle time rises. The first corrective act: (A) add capacity ·
   (B) reduce WIP limits · (C) enlarge stories · (D) skip reviews
   *(QB-U5-03)*
3. A BATNA is: (A) your public demand · (B) your best alternative to no
   agreement — the leverage source · (C) the midpoint · (D) their offer
   *(QB-U5-07)*

**Section B — Scenario short answer (5 marks).**

4. A team commits 21 points into a sprint with 18.6 points of capacity.
   What happens by day 7, and what are the two corrections available at
   planning? *(QB-U5-09)*

**Marking:** mid-sprint carryover + goal erosion predicted (3); corrections
— move a story out / split and reorder by goal contribution; never extend
the sprint (2).

## Version B

**Section A — MCQ (3 × 1 mark).**

1. A sprint backlog is: (A) the PO's list · (B) the Developers' plan for one
   sprint · (C) bugs only · (D) value-ordered only *(QB-U5-02)*
2. A hypothesis backlog item is strongest when it: (A) names an
   architecture · (B) states a measurable prediction to confirm/refute ·
   (C) lists hyperparameters · (D) comes from the loudest stakeholder
   *(QB-U5-04)*
3. A Scrum-of-Scrums primarily: (A) reports status upward · (B) coordinates
   cross-team dependencies · (C) replaces planning · (D) assesses HR
   *(QB-U5-05)*

**Section B — Scenario short answer (5 marks).**

4. A sponsor approves nothing in under three weeks; the team treats silence
   as consent. Name and apply the mechanism from L26. *(QB-U5-15)*

**Marking:** default-with-notice named (2); applied concretely — one-page
memo, stated default, by-date ("if no objection by Thursday, we proceed
with B") (2); why it works — silence becomes a watched decision, sponsor
load drops to veto-only (1).

## Marking guide (both versions)

- Scenario items: award for mechanism + application, not prose quality;
  a correct mechanism misapplied to the scenario caps at 2/5.
- Debrief at L26: item A/3 (or B/1) — most students still equate
  negotiation with the demand, not the alternative.
- Boundary: 4/5 for a correct answer missing the "why it works" layer.
