## Section B — Q7 *(marking criteria)*
Offer: verified position within two weeks — a decision pack with options
(1). Refuse: no commitments or re-baseline before assessment (1). Service
argument: a truthful baseline makes her later wins real; a cosmetic one
makes her third quarter impossible (1).

## Section B — Q8 *(marking criteria)*
Default-with-notice named (1); concrete application — one-page memo, stated
default and by-date ("no objection by Thursday → we proceed with B") (1);
works because silence becomes a watched decision and sponsor load drops to
veto-only (1).

## Section C — Q9 *(marking criteria)*
Obligations: professional (honesty compels correction), regulatory (false
submission is organizational exposure), employment (pressure mitigates for
the person, never the act) (2, ⅔ each). Sequence: verify facts same day,
then seek 24-hour correction from the test lead (2). Award full marks for
correction-first sequences; penalize regulator-first only if internal
correction is skipped entirely, and debrief why (CS-90's alternatives note).
