## MCQ items

### QB-U1-01 [L01 · CLO1 · Remember · F · MCQ] (1 mark)
Which statement best describes the **iron triangle**?
A. Scope, cost, and quality traded against each other on fixed schedule
B. Sponsor, PM, and team — the three governance roles
C. Initiation, planning, and closure — the first three process groups
D. Time, cost, and quality — the three sides of PMBOK 7's value triangle

**Answer: A.** (D is the trap: PMBOK 7's value triangle is value, not the
classic constraint triple.) Quiz1 vA item 1.

### QB-U1-02 [L01 · CLO1 · Understand · F · MCQ] (1 mark)
A project delivers on time and on budget but the sponsor's operation never
uses the output. In the course's success framework this is:
A. A success — constraints met
B. A partial failure — output produced, outcome failed
C. A failure of estimation only
D. Out of scope for project management

**Answer: B.** Marks the distinction between project success (constraints)
and product/value success (outcomes). Ties to CS-02 (success/failure twins).

### QB-U1-03 [L02 · CLO1 · Understand · F · MCQ] (1 mark)
Which is best classified as a **program**?
A. Replacing one server
B. A multi-year regulatory compliance initiative coordinating six projects
C. Monthly payroll processing
D. One team's two-week sprint

**Answer: B.** Coordinated projects pursuing strategic benefits = program;
operations (C) and projects (A, D) excluded. CS-06 vignette logic.

### QB-U1-04 [L02 · CLO1 · Apply · I · MCQ] (1 mark)
Portfolio management differs from program management primarily because it:
A. Executes projects directly
B. Selects, prioritizes, and balances components against strategy and capacity
C. Manages interdependencies only
D. Certifies delivery quality

**Answer: B.**

### QB-U1-05 [L03 · CLO1 · Remember · F · MCQ] (1 mark)
PMBOK 7 is organized around:
A. 49 processes in five groups
B. 12 principles and 8 performance domains
C. 10 knowledge areas
D. Three maturity levels

**Answer: B.**

### QB-U1-06 [L03 · CLO1 · Apply · I · MCQ] (1 mark)
A burndown chart is best mapped to which performance domain?
A. Planning
B. Delivery
C. Measurement
D. Stakeholder

**Answer: C (Measurement).** Burndown tracks progress against plan —
measurement; artifacts exercise drill is CS-04.

### QB-U1-07 [L04 · CLO1 · Understand · F · MCQ] (1 mark)
The project sponsor's core accountability is:
A. Writing the WBS
B. Owning the business case and funding decisions
C. Assigning daily tasks
D. Running standups

**Answer: B.**

### QB-U1-08 [L04 · CLO1 · Apply · I · MCQ] (1 mark)
In a RACI chart, having **two Accountables** for one deliverable signals:
A. Healthy redundancy
B. A governance defect — decision rights are ambiguous
C. A matrix organization
D. Shared best practice

**Answer: B.**

## Short-answer items

### QB-U1-09 [L01 · CLO1 · Analyze · I · short] (5 marks)
A data-science pilot "failed" because the model never deployed, but the team
learned which two features carry no signal. Argue — in under 150 words —
whether this project failed, referencing the iron triangle and the
outcome/output distinction.

**Marking guide:** names constraint triangle (1); distinguishes output
(undeployed model) from outcome/learning (1); recognizes learning artifacts
can be success criteria if the charter defined them (2); conclusion consistent
with argument, not absolute (1). Case anchor: CS-01/CS-02.

### QB-U1-10 [L02 · CLO1 · Analyze · I · short] (5 marks)
A faculty IT group runs: (1) a network upgrade, (2) a research-data catalog,
(3) the annual exam timetable, (4) a two-year digital-learning initiative
containing three projects. Classify each as project / program / operations
and give one reason per classification.

**Answer:** (1) project — temporary, unique output; (2) project (or ongoing
operations if continuously curated — accept with reason); (3) operations —
recurring; (4) program — coordinates multiple projects toward strategic
benefit. 1 mark each + 1 for reasoning quality.

### QB-U1-11 [L03 · CLO1 · Apply · I · short] (5 marks)
Give one PMBOK 7 principle and explain, with a data-science example, how it
changes a concrete PM decision.

**Marking guide:** correct principle named (1); example is genuinely
data-science-specific, not generic (2); decision change is concrete and
caused by the principle (2). Accept e.g. stewardship → consent-scope check
before feature engineering (L28 link); systems thinking → model + serving +
monitoring planned together.

### QB-U1-12 [L04 · CLO1 · Evaluate · A · case] (6 marks)
**Case anchor: CS-05 (RACI for a campus portal team).** A portal deliverable
has the PM as Accountable and the tech lead also Accountable. The CCB approves
changes through the tech lead only. Diagnose the two governance defects and
propose the corrected decision-rights assignment.

**Marking criteria:** dual-A defect named with consequence (decision stalls or
conflicts) (2); CCB-through-tech-lead defect named (change control captured by
delivery role — self-approval) (2); corrected assignment: PM/BA Accountable
for deliverable, CCB independent of delivery with sponsor delegate (2).

### QB-U1-13 [L02 · CLO1 · Evaluate · A · case] (6 marks)
**Case anchor: CS-03 (Portfolio cut-line exercise).** Explain why the
highest-scoring candidate project can rationally be excluded from a funded
portfolio, and name the tiebreaker rule that resolves equal-value bundles.

**Marking criteria:** budget-consumption argument (top score ≠ best value per
rupee or per bundle) (3); knapsack reasoning — bundle value, not item rank
(2); value-per-million (marginal value) tiebreaker (1).

### QB-U1-14 [L01 · CLO1 · Understand · F · short] (3 marks)
State the three sides of the iron triangle and one way each is measured.

**Answer:** scope (requirements delivered / change count), time (schedule
variance), cost (CPI or budget variance). 1 mark per side+measure.

### QB-U1-15 [L03 · CLO1 · Understand · F · short] (3 marks)
Why does PMBOK 7 replace prescriptive processes with principles and domains?
Answer in ≤ 80 words.

**Marking guide:** tailoring/principles-over-processes argument (2);
domains as delivery lenses not phases (1).

### QB-U1-16 [L04 · CLO1 · Apply · I · short] (4 marks)
Name the four roles in the course's project governance picture and one
decision each owns.

**Answer (accept equivalents):** sponsor — funding/business-case decisions;
PM — plan, prioritization, delivery decisions; team — technical estimates and
approach; CCB — baseline changes. 1 mark per role+decision.

### QB-U1-17 [L02 · CLO1 · Analyze · A · case] (6 marks)
**Case anchor: CS-95 (Portfolio rebalance under the mandate).** A regulatory
mandate consumes 1.2M with no strategic score. Give two governance safeguards
a portfolio board should apply when mandates enter the pipeline.

**Marking criteria:** force mandates to state regulatory basis + impact
deadline (3); force explicit accounting of what the mandate displaces
(dead-zone projects) or re-open the scoring of committed projects (3).

### QB-U1-18 [L01 · CLO1 · Evaluate · A · case] (5 marks)
**Case anchor: CS-01 (Iron-triangle autopsy).** A failed ERP rollout met its
scope but doubled its schedule and budget. Which single governance mechanism,
installed at initiation, would most likely have prevented the outcome? Defend
the choice against one alternative.

**Marking criteria:** plausible mechanism with initiation-stage placement
(stage-gate with kill criteria / realistic baseline sign-off / contingency
policy) (3); explicit comparison with one alternative (2). No single right
answer — grade the defense.
