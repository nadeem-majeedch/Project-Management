# Rubric — Capstone Master Plan (part of A7)

Four levels per dimension: 0 inadequate · 1 developing · 2 proficient · 3 exemplary.

| Dimension | Weight | 2 (proficient) | 3 (exemplary) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Completeness | 20% | all baselines + subsidiary plans present | plus data-quality plan and closure checklist, context-appropriate |
| Internal consistency | 20% | schedule/resources/budget reconcile | risk↔contingency↔reserve traceable line-by-line |
| Tailoring justification | 15% | cuts identified and reasoned | cuts tied to specific project attributes |
| Risk realism | 15% | responses owned, triggered | quantified EMV/Monte Carlo where data allows |
| Communication | 15% | readable in one pass by sponsor | sponsor-ready summary + navigation |
| Team process evidence | 10% | real board/sprint history | history shows adaptation over time |
| Individual contribution | 5% | memo consistent with evidence | peer-verified distinctive contribution |

## Consistency stress tests (run before awarding ≥ 2 on consistency)

1. Critical path drives the Gantt; milestone dates match network output.
2. Cost baseline totals match bottom-up sheet + contingency.
3. Every ≥ 15-score risk has an owner, trigger, response, residual.
4. Communication matrix covers every power/interest quadrant stakeholder.
