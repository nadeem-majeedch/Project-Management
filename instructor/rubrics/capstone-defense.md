# Rubric — Capstone Defense (part of A7)

Per-team, on four levels: 0 inadequate · 1 developing · 2 proficient · 3 exemplary.

| Dimension | Weight | 2 (proficient) | 3 (exemplary) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plan quality under questioning | 30% | plan defended accurately | defended with trade-offs, alternatives named |
| Q&A judgment | 30% | sensible responses to probes | reframes flawed questions, quantifies claims |
| Time discipline | 15% | 10 min ± 30 s | each section lands in its slot |
| Team coordination | 15% | balanced speaking roles | handovers seamless, no gaps |
| Ethics & AI disclosure | 10% | disclosure present | norms applied to concrete course decisions |

## Question bank seeds (probe judgment, not recall)

- "Your SPI is 0.85 at month 3 — walk me through your first two actions."
- "Which estimate would you re-derive first, and with what technique?"
- "Why accept rather than transfer this risk? What changes your mind?"
- "Show where AI helped and where you rejected its output."

## Scoring logistics

Defense at L32; peer scores collected separately (feedback only, not grade);
absence = 0 for A7 without documented cause (published rubric).
