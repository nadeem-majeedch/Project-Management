# Rubric — Case Portfolio (A6, 10%)

Per-analysis score; 12 analyses required. Four levels per dimension:
0 inadequate · 1 developing · 2 proficient · 3 exemplary.

| Dimension | Weight | 2 (proficient) | 3 (exemplary) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Concept accuracy | 30% | correct use of course concepts, right standard cited | concepts connected across lectures |
| Technique application | 30% | technique applied correctly to the scenario | technique adapted with justification |
| Assumption honesty | 20% | assumptions explicit and reasonable | sensitivity of conclusions to assumptions tested |
| Communication | 20% | clear 300–500 words, structured | conclusion-first, readable in one pass |

## Administration

- ≥ 3 analyses per unit boundary (L08, L14, L20, L26); no two from the same domain.
- Grading: spot-grade 4 of 12 in depth (rotating), remainder pass/refine;
  final portfolio grade uses the 4 deep grades + breadth compliance.
- Integrity: templated/recycled prose → 0 for the analysis; second offense
  escalates per university policy.
