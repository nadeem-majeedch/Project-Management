One plan, every artifact, zero contradictions

Learning objectives

  • Assemble the master plan from the term's artifacts in dependency order
  • Run consistency checks across artifacts (the red-team's first pass)
  • Integrate data-governance and responsible-AI requirements into the plan's spine
  • Defend an integrated plan against a structured red-team
    • Speaker notes
    This is the capstone's assembly lecture — M4 (plan due L27, defended L32) is built here. The red-team checklist mirrors instructor/rubrics/capstone-plan.md. Timing ~5 min.

    The integration map

    flowchart TD CH["Charter (L06)"] --> ST["Stakeholder register (L07)"] ST --> SCO["Scope & WBS (L09)"] SCO --> EST["Estimates (L10)"] EST --> NET["Network & schedule (L11–L12)"] NET --> BUD["Cost baseline (L13)"] BUD --> S["S-curve = PV"] EST --> Q["Quality & DQ rules (L14)"] SCO --> R["Risk register (L15–L17)"] R --> CONT["Contingency → budget"] BUD --> EVM["Monitoring (L19–L20)"] Q --> EVM
  • Every arrow is a consistency claim: estimates feed network feeds budget feeds PV
  • The plan is integrated when changing one artifact forces the updates downstream
    • Speaker notes
    Walk the two chains: scope→money and risk→money. The contingency arrow is where student plans break most. 5 min.

    The red-team's first six checks

  • 100% rule — WBS children sum; nothing outside the WBS
  • Estimate chain — package hours → labor → budget, no orphan numbers
  • Schedule↔budget — S-curve dates match network dates
  • Risk↔contingency — top EMV risks have responses funded by contingency
  • Success criteria — every charter objective has a verifiable criterion (L06)
  • Governance — data rules (L14) and gates (L23) have owners and dates
    • Speaker notes
    These six are the peer-review instrument (docs/capstone/peer-review.md) — students red-team each other's plans today. 5 min.

    Data governance in the plan's spine

  • Consent & lawful basis — stated in the charter's success criteria where personal data is involved
  • Data-quality rules — owned (L07's owners), scheduled (in the WBS), monitored (L20)
  • Model-risk gate — dated in the schedule, evidence defined (L23)
  • Responsible AI is not a policy appendix: it is WBS rows, budget lines, and gate evidence
    • Speaker notes
    This slide is the CLO6 core: governance as artifacts, not aspirations. The DS capstone track requires the model-risk gate explicitly (docs/capstone/specification.md). 4 min.

    CS / DS in the room

  • CS: the campus-portal plan — auth service slip forces the network, then the budget, then the benefits case to move together
  • DS: the AGROSENSE-style yield-model plan — consent scope changes ripple through WBS, gates, and fairness evidence
  • Integration is the plan's property, not its format — a pretty deck with orphan numbers is not a plan
