Using the tools professionally — with verification as the default

    The verification discipline

    flowchart LR D["AI draft"] --> C["Check claims<br>against project facts"] C --> V["Verify numbers<br>(recompute: L19's machine)"] V --> A["Assign owners<br>& provenance"] A --> S["Sign it —<n>your name, your artifact"]
  • If you cannot verify it, you cannot ship it — the artifact's signature is yours
  • AI-drafted ≠ AI-owned: registers, plans, and decisions have human owners (L04's accountability)
    • Speaker notes
    The sign-it step is the professional line this course draws — consistent with the syllabus AI policy's attribution requirement. 4 min.

    Critiquing an AI-drafted risk register (CS-42's method)

  • Specificity: statements without cause–event–effect structure (L15) are only skeletons
  • Coverage: what would a human hunt have found that the draft didn't? (silent stakeholders, data access)
  • Arithmetic: recompute every P×I and EMV — drafts hallucinate products
  • Ownership: names that don't match your org are a gift — they reveal the draft's fiction
  • Threshold integrity: bands that don't match your declared policy (CS-21's lesson)
    • Speaker notes
    The five-check critique is the package's review instrument and CS-42's rubric. It turns the whole term's tools into a review checklist. 5 min.
