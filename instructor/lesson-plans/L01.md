## Talking points

### 1. The project landscape in software & data

- Project success/failure patterns in the software industry
- Famous failures vs famous successes; what the differences trace back to

### 2. The iron triangle and its limits

- Scope, time, cost, and quality as competing constraints
- Why the triangle alone does not explain failure

### 3. What a project manager actually does

- From 'note-taker' to integrator of scope, risk, and stakeholders
- Value of method vs value of judgment

## Cases to run

- CS-01: Iron-triangle autopsy of a failed ERP rollout (level L1, uses in-class, discussion)
- CS-02: Success vs failure twin projects (level L2, uses in-class, discussion)

## Activities to facilitate

- Failure autopsy: small groups map a well-known failure onto the iron triangle
- Baseline quiz: what would you have done first?
