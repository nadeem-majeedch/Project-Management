## Talking points

### 1. From processes to principles

- Why PMI moved from 49 processes (PMBOK 6) to 12 principles (PMBOK 7)
- Principle groups — stewardship, team, stakeholders, value, systems thinking, leadership, tailoring, quality, complexity, risk, adaptability, resilience

### 2. The 8 performance domains

- Stakeholders, team, development approach & life cycle, planning, project work, delivery, measurement, uncertainty
- How domains interact on a real project

### 3. Models, methods, artifacts

- PMBOK 7's 'choose your tools' stance and what it means for tailoring
