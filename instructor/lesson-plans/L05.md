## Talking points

### 1. Life-cycle spectrum

- Predictive (waterfall), iterative, incremental, adaptive, hybrid
- When requirements uncertainty chooses the approach for you

### 2. Decision aids

- Stacey matrix; requirements volatility; regulatory constraints
- Data projects vs software projects vs platform projects

### 3. Sequencing and flow

- Phase gates vs sprints vs continuous flow
