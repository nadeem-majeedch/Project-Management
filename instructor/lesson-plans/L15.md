## Talking points

### 1. Risk vocabulary & process

- Individual vs overall project risk; threat vs opportunity
- ISO 31000 process — scope/context/criteria, assessment, treatment, monitoring

### 2. Identification techniques

- Checklists, interviews, assumption analysis, SWOT, prompt lists (PESTLE, VUCA)

### 3. Qualitative analysis

- Probability/impact scales; risk data quality; heat maps

## Cases to run
