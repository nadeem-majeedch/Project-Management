## Talking points

### 1. Business case essentials

- Problem, options, benefits, costs, risks; do-nothing baseline
- SMART benefit statements for data projects

### 2. Project charter

- Authority, objectives, high-level scope, milestones, sponsor signature
- Charter anti-patterns (vague objectives, unowned assumptions)

### 3. From case to charter

- Traceability from benefits to objectives to success criteria
