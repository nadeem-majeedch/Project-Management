## Talking points

### 1. Tailoring logic

- Project size, criticality, regulatory exposure, team distribution
- What to scale down (documents) vs never scale down (risk, quality)

### 2. Software-project tailoring

- Startup vs enterprise vs regulated (health/finance) contexts

### 3. Data-project tailoring

- Data availability risk; model-uncertainty milestones; experiment-driven planning
