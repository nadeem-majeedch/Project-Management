# Capstone Rubric

Scored on four levels per dimension: **0 inadequate · 1 developing ·
2 proficient · 3 exemplary**. Weighted 25% of the course grade.

| Dimension | Weight | Exemplary looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Completeness | 20% | all baselines + subsidiary plans present; nothing “TODO” |
| Internal consistency | 20% | schedule ↔ resources ↔ budget ↔ risk ↔ contingency all reconcile; red-team issues closed |
| Tailoring justification | 15% | every cut from heavyweight practice defended by project context |
| Risk realism | 15% | responses owned and triggered; quantified where data allows |
| Communication | 15% | plan readable by sponsor in one pass; defense within time |
| Team process evidence | 10% | board/sprint history shows real cadence, not backfill |
| Individual contribution | 5% | memo consistent with evidence |

## Gates

- Defense attendance is mandatory; absence = 0 for A7 without documented cause.
- The plan draft (L27) must exist before the defense; skipping the draft
  forfeits the red-team feedback loop and caps *internal consistency* at 1.

## Academic integrity

All data synthetic or public; all standards cited by edition/year; AI
assistance disclosed per course policy and defended in Q&A.
