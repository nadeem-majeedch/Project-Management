# Capstone Presentation Rubric (defense, part of A7)

Four levels per dimension: **0 inadequate · 1 developing · 2 proficient ·
3 exemplary**. Scored by the instructor; peer scores are feedback only.
The numeric conversion from rubric levels to the A7 percentage is an
**instructor decision** announced with the [grading](../assessments/grading.md)
policy — it is not silently defaulted here.

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Weight | 2 (proficient) | 3 (exemplary) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plan quality under questioning | 30% | plan defended accurately | defended with trade-offs; alternatives named and priced |
| Q&A judgment | 30% | sensible responses to probes | reframes flawed questions; quantifies claims on demand |
| Time discipline | 15% | 10 min ± 30 s | each section lands in its slot |
| Team coordination | 15% | balanced speaking roles | seamless handovers; no gaps or overlaps |
| Ethics & AI disclosure | 10% | disclosure present | norms applied to concrete plan decisions |

## What the Q&A probes look like

Questions test **judgment under the plan's own numbers**:

- "Your SPI is 0.85 at month 3 — walk me through your first two actions."
- "Why accept rather than transfer this risk? What evidence changes your mind?"
- "Which estimate would you re-derive first, and with what technique?"
- "Your contingency is X. What is it protecting against that your schedule
  buffer doesn't already cover?"
- "Show where AI helped this plan and where you rejected its output."

*"The template said so" is not a defense. Neither is "the tool generated it."*

## Level anchors for the two heavy dimensions

**Plan quality — 0:** artifact missing or contradicts itself under the first
probe. **1:** present but read from, not known — answers recite the slide.
**2:** accurate and consistent; trade-offs acknowledged when asked.
**3:** trade-offs volunteered; the decision log appendix is used live;
alternatives carry prices and dates.

**Q&A — 0:** deflects or improvises facts. **1:** correct but shallow;
retreats under a second probe. **2:** defends with plan evidence; concedes
unknowns honestly. **3:** reframes the question when the premise is flawed;
answers carry numbers from the plan without looking for them.

## Session logistics

- 10 minutes presenting + 5 minutes Q&A per team; the timekeeper cuts off at
  11:00 — content after the cut scores 0 on time discipline.
- All members present and speaking; absence rules per
  [grading](../assessments/grading.md) constraints.
- Deliverables at the door: plan PDF, slides, process-evidence export;
  individual memos privately via LMS ([milestones](milestones.md) M4).

## How to rehearse

1. Run the 10-minute outline against a visible timer twice minimum; the
   outline order is fixed by the [defense format](defense-format.md).
2. Red-team each other with the five probe types above until answers come
   without slides.
3. Assign one member as the "consistency owner" — every number quoted in
   the room must match the plan PDF; that person says so when it does.
