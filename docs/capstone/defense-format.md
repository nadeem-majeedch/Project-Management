# Capstone Defense Format

**Session:** lecture 32, week 16 · **Per team:** 10 minutes presenting +
5 minutes Q&A · rubric-scored by instructor (peers score separately for
feedback only).

## Required 10-minute outline

| Minutes | Content |
|---|---|
| 0–1 | Project, sponsor, why it matters |
| 1–3 | Scope + schedule baseline (show the critical path) |
| 3–4 | Cost baseline + contingency logic |
| 4–6 | Top risks, responses, triggers |
| 6–8 | Tailoring: what you cut and why |
| 8–10 | Ethics, sustainability, AI-use disclosure |

## Q&A expectations

Questions probe **judgment**: "your SPI is 0.85 at month 3 — what do you do?",
"why accept rather than transfer this risk?", "which estimate would you
re-derive first?". Teams defend choices; “the template said so” is not a
defense.

## Deliverable checklist (due at defense)

- [ ] Final master plan (PDF)
- [ ] Defense slides (≤ 12)
- [ ] Process evidence export (board/sprint history)
- [ ] Individual contribution memos (private, LMS)
