# Capstone Milestone Schedule

Four graded checkpoints keep the capstone from becoming a week-16 emergency.
Each has a defined artifact and consequence; dates map to the lecture
schedule.

| # | Milestone | When | Artifact due | If missed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Team + project registration | **L08** | Team roster (3–5), track choice, one-paragraph project brief | Selection after L08 needs instructor approval and forfeits early-feedback |
| M2 | Charter approved | **L12** | [Charter](capstone-charter.md) — SMART objectives, success criteria, sponsor role | M3 feedback has no anchor; charter rework lands on the same deadline as M3 |
| M3 | Plan draft — red-team exchange | **L27** | Full draft of all 12 artifacts (templates allowed to be partially filled where data is pending, but no missing sections) | Skipping the draft forfeits the red-team loop and **caps internal consistency at level 1** (rubric gate) |
| M4 | Final submission + defense | **L32** | Master plan PDF, slides ≤ 12, process-evidence export, individual memos (private, LMS) | Defense absence = 0 for A7 without documented cause |

## What each checkpoint checks

**M1 — viability.** Instructor review of scope realism: is the project big
enough to exercise all 12 artifacts, small enough to plan genuinely in 8
weeks? One-paragraph brief is rejected, not silently accepted — expect a
response within one week.

**M2 — direction.** The charter is graded pass/refine against SMART
criteria. A refined charter must be resubmitted within one week; the
objectives fixed here become the defense's success-criteria questions.

**M3 — integration pressure.** Drafts are exchanged between teams in a
structured **red-team session**: each team attacks another's plan with the
consistency stress tests (network↔Gantt, budget reconciliation, risk
coverage, communication-matrix completeness) and hands back a written issue
list. Issues must be **closed and evidenced** in the final submission —
the rubric's consistency dimension rewards closed red-team issues, and the
defense will probe the two hardest ones.

**M4 — the defense.** 10 minutes + 5 minutes Q&A per the
[defense format](defense-format.md), scored on the
[rubric](capstone-rubric.md). Peer scores are feedback only.

## Workload pacing (from the assessment strategy)

| Weeks | Capstone effort expected |
|---|---|
| 3–7 (M1–M2) | ~1 h/week — registration, charter iterations alongside labs |
| 8–12 | ~2 h/week — artifacts build alongside A2–A4 submissions |
| 13–14 (M3) | 4–6 h — draft assembly and the red-team exchange |
| 15–16 (M4) | 5–8 h — issue closure, slide build, defense rehearsal |

Peak capstone load (weeks 13–16) deliberately follows the A2–A4 deadline
cluster — plan accordingly.
