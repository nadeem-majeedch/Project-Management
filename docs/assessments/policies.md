# Course Policies

## Late work

5% deduction per 24 hours late, up to a 5-day ceiling. The capstone plan
draft (L27) accepts no late submissions — it feeds peer review.

## Missed quizzes

Quizzes run best-2-of-3, which absorbs one miss without documentation. A
second miss requires documented justification and may convert weight to the
final exam.

## Collaboration & integrity

- Assignments are individual unless the brief marks them otherwise; the
  capstone is a team deliverable with an individual accountability memo.
- **AI tools** may be used for drafting and exploration **with disclosure**;
  fabricating data, sources, or results is an integrity violation. Course
  norms are co-written during L31 and apply to all submissions.
- Cite standards by edition and year (e.g., *PMBOK Guide 7, PMI, 2021*).
- The case portfolio is individual writing; templated or recycled analyses
  are trivially detected and heavily penalized.

## Re-grade requests

Within one week of return, in writing, citing the specific rubric line.

## Attendance

Activities are graded through participation in anchored cases; there is no
separate attendance grade, but case work cannot be recovered outside class.

## Communication

Course announcements go through the LMS; questions about content go to the
unit discussion thread; personal matters to the instructor's email.
