# Quiz Guide (A1 — 10%, best 2 of 3)

Three 15-minute quizzes run at the **start of lecture** on L04, L12, and
L26. Your **best two scores count**; the lowest is dropped at final grade
computation — one bad day is absorbed without documentation. A second miss
requires documented justification and may convert weight to the final exam
([policies](policies.md)).

## Format and coverage

| Quiz | When | Coverage | Format |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quiz 1 | start of L04 | L01–L04 (CLO1) | 4 MCQ + 1 short (7 marks) |
| Quiz 2 | start of L12 | L09–L12 (CLO2) | 3 MCQ + 1 computation (8 marks) |
| Quiz 3 | start of L26 | L21–L26 (CLO5) | 3 MCQ + 1 scenario (8 marks) |

Everything is drawn from published lecture pages and case briefs — nothing
rides on undisclosed content. Quiz 2's computation is CPM-network arithmetic
of exactly the kind practiced in [Lab 4](../labs/lab-04-cpm-network.md) and
case [CS-15](../cases/CS-15.md).

## How to prepare

1. **Reread the four lecture pages** in the quiz's range — the "After this
   lecture" checklist on each page is the recall layer.
2. **Redo one worked example with the book closed:** the CPM pass for
   Quiz 2, the capacity chain for any sprint question.
3. **Re-derive, don't re-read formulas:** the quiz tests application; if you
   can run a forward/backward pass on a fresh 8-activity network, Quiz 2 is
   safe.
4. Quiz 1's known trap: the PMBOK 7 value triangle is **value**, not the
   iron-triangle constraint triple.

## After the quiz

Papers are returned within one week with item-level feedback. The most-missed
item is debriefed at the next lecture — treat that debrief as part of the
learning, not the post-mortem.
