# Labs

Sixteen substantial labs — one per teaching week from week 3, with doubled
coverage in the artifact-dense weeks — plus lecture exercises inside the
teaching material. Labs 1–16 share one educational scenario,
**[CAMPUS-MEND](data-pack.md)** (a university portal rebuild), so every
artifact you build feeds the next; the workbook ends where the
[capstone](../capstone/index.md) begins.

Every calculation lab has independently checkable answers; completed
worksheets are verified by the course answer checker, and model solutions
stay with the instructor.

## Rules

- Labs are individual, submitted via LMS within one week of the lab session.
- Graded **pass / refine** against the checklist in each brief; the
  [assessment strategy](../assessments/assessment-strategy.md) explains how
  labs feed the capstone.
- **Integrity:** the [data pack](data-pack.md) is educational data. For
  capstone work on your own project, cite the pack if you reuse its numbers,
  and never submit another student's artifacts. AI assistance follows the
  [responsible-AI contract](../assessments/academic-integrity.md).
