# Lab 2 — Stakeholders & Power–Interest

## 1. Objectives
1. Build a register that captures *exchanges* (what each side needs from the other), not just names.
2. Assess power and interest with cited evidence; place every stakeholder exactly once on the grid.
3. Close engagement gaps with concrete actions (who does what, by when).

## 2. Background & scenario
The 2018 portal failed partly for *human* reasons: the student union heard
about the outage from social media, the LMS vendor learned about integration
changes from a forwarded email, and the exams office was told results would
be "on time" until the night they weren't. CAMPUS-MEND inherits all these
people. From the [data pack story](data-pack.md), identify at least ten
stakeholders — including at least one who is *not* a person (a rule, a vendor
contract, a governing body) but behaves like one.

## 3. Required tools
- [Stakeholder register template](../templates/stakeholder-register-template.md) (register + ASCII grid)
- [Data pack](data-pack.md)
- Case [CS-06](../cases/CS-06.md) read in the lecture (engagement levels)

## 4. Step-by-step instructions
1. **Brainstorm (15 min).** List every stakeholder the story implies. Pairs compare lists; add the ones you missed (typical misses: exams office, hostels, students as *users* vs union as *voice*).
2. **Exchange columns first (20 min).** For each: what they need from us / what we need from them. If a row has no exchange, it's an audience, not a stakeholder — mark it as such.
3. **Score P and I (20 min).** 1–5 each. Any 4 or 5 requires a one-line evidence citation (a decision they own, a contract clause, a demonstrated behavior).
4. **Plot the grid (10 min).** Every ID exactly once. Low interest + high power stakeholders are the dangerous quadrant — check you have identified at least one.
5. **Engagement gaps (20 min).** Current → Desired levels; actions for every gap, with owner and timing.
6. **Refine (out of lab, 1 h).** Reassess-after-gates note: state *when* scores will move (e.g., "exams office → leading at G4").

## 5. Your tasks (checklist)
- [ ] ≥ 10 stakeholders incl. ≥ 1 non-person entity
- [ ] Exchange columns filled for every row
- [ ] P/I scores with evidence for all 4s and 5s
- [ ] Grid: every ID exactly once; monitor quadrant not empty
- [ ] Engagement-gap plan with owners and dates
- [ ] Reassessment triggers stated (which gate moves which score)

## 6. Expected outputs
`stakeholders.md` — register table, grid diagram, gap plan. Submit via LMS.

## 7. Reflection questions
1. Which stakeholder did your pair *both* miss initially — and what does that omission pattern tell you about where registers fail?
2. Name one stakeholder whose *interest* score will change after week 8. What event moves it, and what will you do before it moves?
3. The LMS vendor is high power, apparently low interest. What is the risk of reading their interest as low — and what evidence would settle it?

## 8. Assessment rubric (pass / refine)
| Dimension | Pass | Refine |
|---|---|---|
| Coverage | ≥ 10 incl. non-obvious entities | obvious names only |
| Exchanges | both directions for all rows | name-and-role lists |
| Scoring | evidence-cited 4s/5s | bare numbers |
| Grid | complete, correct quadrants | several IDs unplaced |
| Gap plan | owned, dated actions | "improve communication" |
