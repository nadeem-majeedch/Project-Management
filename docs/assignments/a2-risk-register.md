# Assignment 2 — Risk Register & Response Plans (A3, 10%)

**CLO4** · Released after L17 · Due after L19 · Individual

## Task

For the project you scoped in Assignment 1 (or a new approved seed):

1. **Identify** ≥ 18 distinct risks using at least three identification
   techniques from L15; include ≥ 2 opportunities and ≥ 2 data/ML-specific
   risks if your project has a data component
2. **Qualify** every risk on a 5×5 probability × impact scale; normalize
   each to a cause-risk-effect statement
3. **Quantify** at least one risk with EMV (decision tree) and justify the
   probability source
4. **Respond** for the top 10: choose strategy (escalate/avoid/mitigate/
   transfer/accept — or exploit/share/enhance/accept), name an owner, define
   a trigger, and state residual risk
5. **Size contingency** for your cost baseline and defend it in ≤ 150 words

## Deliverables & weight guide

| Artifact | Weight |
|---|---|
| Register (18+ risks, normalized, qualified) | 35% |
| EMV analysis | 15% |
| Response plans (top 10, complete fields) | 35% |
| Contingency sizing + defense | 15% |

## Integrity

Registries drafted with AI assistance must disclose it and will be probed
in viva-style questions during the following lab.
