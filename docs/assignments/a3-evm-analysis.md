# Assignment 3 — EVM Case Analysis (A4, 10%)

**CLO4** · Released after L19 · Due after L21 · Individual

## Task

You receive a monthly status pack for a troubled project (distributed via
LMS; based on a modified exam-bank scenario): PV/EV/AC at months 1–5,
work-package detail, and a change log.

1. Compute the **full EVM set** at month 5: PV, EV, AC, CV, SV, CPI, SPI,
   BAC, and all four EAC variants with ETC and TCPI
2. **Diagnose** the project in ≤ 400 words: what do the indices say, what do
   they hide (name at least two measurement traps present in the data)
3. **Recover** the schedule: compare crashing vs fast-tracking for this
   project with cost and risk implications
4. **Design** one milestone-based EV rule that would make future monthly
   reporting harder to game

## Deliverables & weight guide

| Artifact | Weight |
|---|---|
| Correct EVM computation | 40% |
| Diagnosis incl. measurement traps | 30% |
| Recovery comparison | 20% |
| EV rule design | 10% |

Show formulas with numbers substituted; unexplained results score zero
regardless of the final index values.
