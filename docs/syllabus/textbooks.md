# Textbooks & References

Every item below is a real, published artifact cited with edition and year.
**No purchase is required** — the university library provides PMI standards
through its subscription; free editions exist for the rest (see notes).

## Required (course backbone)

| # | Reference | Role in the course | Access |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | PMI. (2021). *A guide to the project management body of knowledge (PMBOK guide)* (7th ed.). Project Management Institute. | Twelve principles + eight performance domains (L03 backbone); domain vocabulary all semester | Library subscription; PMI site offers a free preview |
| 2 | Schwaber, K., & Sutherland, J. (2020). *The Scrum Guide*. Scrum.org. | Scrum mechanics in L21; the 2020 edition is the current one | **Free** at scrum.org |

## Citation format required in coursework

> Body. (Year). *Title* (Edition). Publisher.

Example: PMI. (2021). *A guide to the project management body of knowledge
(PMBOK guide)* (7th ed.). Project Management Institute.

For standards: ISO 31000:2018 — include title and ISO as publisher. For web
sources (Scrum Guide, Fountain, Fowler): include the URL and access date.
